# One product, one reference

T—01's hero is assembled from photographic parts, all aligned to the same approved 1448 × 1086 product composition. The finished web interaction preserves that geometry instead of placing new illustrated controls over it.

## Parts

- `session-minimal-v8.webp`: approved assembled product; source for the three key crops, body legends and the arm.
- `session-clean-v8.webp`: same chassis with the arm removed, to expose the surface under its movement.
- `session-disc-v8.webp`: overhead circular record surface with a centered pin, rotated inside a fixed projection.
- `session-face-v8.webp`: overhead circular dial face, reused across all five dials. Only the top rotates; the photographed barrels remain stationary.
- `session-blue-v8.webp`: matching studio composition for the cobalt section.
- `session-lifestyle-v8.webp`: matching product in a warm desk setting.
- `session-controls-macro-v4.webp`: photographic macro of the same three-key transport and main dial.

These are generated concept images, not photographs of a manufactured prototype.

## Geometry that must remain stable

The controls are percentage positions derived from the assembled photo, so every layer scales together. The four front dials share one vertical center. The large dial face uses its own fixed foreshortening ratio. The disc is clipped to a true circle before a fixed projection; the central pin is part of the same centered image.

Do not spin a photograph of a dial barrel, an oblique ellipse or a whole chassis. Do not let changing rotation alter the projected silhouette. Crop opaque source images inside their clean circular material region; the source exterior is not a transparent background.

The expression arm is masked from the assembled reference and rotates around its measured pivot. The touch region sits on the grip; the visual arm itself is not an invisible full-page interaction layer.

Inspect changes the viewfinder's scale and origin while retaining the same coordinates. `overflow: clip` prevents focus from panning an accidental scroll container. At phone sizes, verify both inspection modes and all four front dial targets.

## Before accepting an image revision

Compare key count, glyphs, dial count, spacing, material, lighting, arm geometry and display location against the master. Review the product at rest and through a full revolution. A beautiful new angle that describes a different device is not a usable reference for this page.

The reference explains an exterior direction. It does not prove enclosure thickness, connector clearance, PCB fit, finger clearance, finish process or real hardware feasibility. Those require CAD, drawings and prototypes.
